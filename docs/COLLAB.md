## How I like to work

- **Small PRs, loud pipelines.** I would rather be told I am wrong by CI in four minutes than by a colleague in four days.
- **Write the runbook with the alert.** If I cannot say what someone should do at 3am, the alert is not finished.
- **Postmortems without blame, with actions.** A postmortem that produces no merged change was a meeting.
- **Documentation lives next to the code.** A wiki page nobody updates is worse than no page.
- **I use AI heavily, and it does not get merge rights.** It narrows the search space during an incident and drafts the RCA; a human still signs off.
